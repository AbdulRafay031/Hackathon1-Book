## Research Findings: Performance Goals for Code Examples/Simulations

### Decision
Performance goals for code examples and simulations in Physical AI & Humanoid Robotics will focus on real-time decision making, accurate simulation, and task generalization.

### Rationale
Effective and safe robot operation in real-world environments necessitates minimal latency for processing sensory data and executing actions. Accurate simulations are crucial for validating control algorithms and testing behaviors before physical deployment. Task generalization, supported by synthetic data from simulations, enables robots to learn and perform a wide variety of tasks efficiently.
